Is Stackt free?
Yes. Stackt is free to play with ads between rounds. A one-time Remove Ads purchase is available in the Shop if you'd rather play ad-free — it's a single payment, not a subscription.
I bought Remove Ads but still see ads / it's not restoring on a new device
Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases — Apple re-links it to your Apple ID automatically. If that doesn't fix it, email us the approximate purchase date and we'll sort it out.
How does the Daily Challenge work?
Everyone gets the same puzzle each day. Complete it to keep your streak alive — a new one unlocks at midnight local time.
Can I play offline?
Yes, the puzzle itself needs no connection. Ads simply won't load without one.
How do I delete my data?
Everything lives on your device only — deleting the app deletes your progress. There's no account or server-side data to remove.
